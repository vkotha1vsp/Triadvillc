Anti-Counterfeit

Verification at the point of scan.

Serialization enables real-time validation against GTIN, serial number, lot, and expiration date — flagging anomalies before they reach a patient.

Counterfeit Detection — Reference Demo

Scan a 2D DataMatrix barcode to validate GTIN, serial number, lot, and expiration date against the serialization repository.

Authentic Product

  • Valid serial number match
  • Expected supply-chain location
  • Active, non-decommissioned status
  • No duplicate scans detected

Potential Counterfeit

  • Unknown or unrecognized serial number
  • Duplicate scan of an existing serial
  • Product scanned in an unexpected region
  • Decommissioned or expired product
